TERMS / PRIVACY

Simple tool. Clear limits.

No account, no cookies, no analytics. Forecasts are decision support, with no warranty.

Where your information goes

Coordinates go from the browser directly to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather and, where needed, a timezone. Place searches also go directly to Open-Meteo. Nothing you enter goes to us. Array capacity, direction, inverter limits and risk settings remain in your browser. Map tiles come directly from OpenStreetMap, which receives tile requests and ordinary request metadata. The model runs on your device.

The site has no backend and does not collect form submissions, set cookies, use analytics, or store your settings in a database. The site host serves static files and may process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses and access logs under its own privacy policy. Open-Meteo receives your IP address along with requests; its terms and privacy policy apply to that service.

“Use my location” asks your browser for permission. You can decline and use city search or typed coordinates. The location is used to request weather directly from Open-Meteo and, if you open the map, to centre the map tiles.

Share links and downloads

Your chosen location, coordinates, arrays, inverter limit, unit, day and risk setting are included in the URL fragment (after #). The fragment is not sent to the static host in HTTP requests. Anyone you send the complete link to can read these settings. Do not share it if the location is sensitive. CSV and JSON downloads are generated locally; they include location or forecast settings where stated.

Saved sites use localStorage on this device. A service worker caches the app shell, downloaded models, recent weather responses and the last calculated forecast for offline use, with a saved-at timestamp. This storage is for the tool, not tracking. Delete saved sites using their cards, or clear the browser’s site data to remove all saved settings and offline copies. If storage is blocked, forecasting and share links still work.

Decision support, not a guarantee

Forecasts and uncertainty bands are estimates provided as-is, with no warranty of accuracy, availability, completeness or fitness for a particular purpose. Weather services, data coverage and models can fail or change. You are responsible for checking the suitability of any forecast before making operational, trading, safety or financial decisions.

The model assumes a typical fleet mix. Up to three arrays apply forecast tilted-irradiance ratios, with a labelled clear-sky fallback. The optional inverter AC limit clips their summed output. Site-specific shading and equipment losses are not modelled. A nominal confidence level is not a promise of actual coverage. Daily energy totals sum interval quantiles and are not calibrated daily energy quantiles. Outside the model's tested geography, treat estimates with particular care. Read the method and measured limitations.

Availability and pricing

The tool, CSV/JSON exports and public ledger are free today. Pro: API and alerts: not available yet. No payment is collected here. Availability and API rate limits depend in part on Open-Meteo; this tool has no service-level guarantee. Commercial use of third-party data may require a separate licence or plan from its provider.
